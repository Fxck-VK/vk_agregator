# Epifera homepage media

The 32 editorial artworks were generated through Epifera with Nano Banana Pro on 2026-11-05. The original portrait was requested at 4K; the other final artworks at 2K. WebP copies retain the source framing and colors; the portrait has a larger display copy to retain skin detail.

The four `ui-` assets are lossless captures of Epifera with prepared public content. Their use on the public homepage was explicitly approved by the owner. They exclude account identity, balances, previous private chats and unrelated files. UI labels are rendered from these exact captures as separate image layers rather than generated text. Model-card artworks are editorial illustrations; they do not claim that the named model generated them.

Original generation files, job evidence, quality holds and budget receipts remain outside Git in the ignored local task cache. No credential, private artifact URL or raw account data belongs here.
